PART I

Item 1. Business

Acme & Co designs and sells industrial widgets to enterprise customers worldwide. We operate two reportable segments: Widgets and Services. Our scale and long-standing brand are durable competitive advantages that we believe are difficult for new entrants to replicate. We sell primarily through a direct salesforce supplemented by regional distributors.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our business is subject to the following risks. The loss of a major customer would materially and adversely affect our results of operations. Changes in regulation could increase our compliance costs. Disruptions in our supply chain, including the concentration of certain components with a single supplier, could impair our ability to fulfill orders. We are also exposed to foreign-currency fluctuations across the markets in which we operate.

Item 2. Properties

We lease our corporate headquarters in Springfield and own three manufacturing facilities.

PART II

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

Revenue grew 12% year over year, driven primarily by unit volume in the Widgets segment. Operating margin expanded 140 basis points as we leveraged fixed costs. We expect continued mid-single-digit volume growth, partially offset by input-cost inflation, over the next fiscal year.
